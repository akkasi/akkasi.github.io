I am a computer scientist specializing in Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, and Machine Learning, with emerging expertise in AI-driven cybersecurity. With a rich international background, he has held academic and research positions in Canada, Europe, and the Middle East, contributing to impactful projects in large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, multimodal AI, and graph-based deep learning. His work bridges academia and industry through innovative AI solutions, interdisciplinary collaboration, and mentoring of future researchers. An award-winning scholar with numerous peer-reviewed publications, Abbas is passionate about advancing inclusive, accessible, and socially impactful AI technologies.
