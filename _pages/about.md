With a rich international background, he has held academic and research positions in Canada, Europe, and the Middle East, contributing to impactful projects in large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, multimodal AI, and graph-based deep learning. His work bridges academia and industry through innovative AI solutions, interdisciplinary collaboration, and mentoring of future researchers.
